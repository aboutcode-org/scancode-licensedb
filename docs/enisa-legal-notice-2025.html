The reproduction of the ENISA logo is permitted provided that the source is acknowledged and the logo is used for press and media related purposes. All other uses are prohibited unless permission is obtained.

1.4  ENISA IPR Policy for the Public

ENISA adopted its IPR policy in August 2021. The public version of this policy is aimed to provide information to all interested parties regarding the use of ENISA’s works, as well as the overall ENISA’s approach to IPR.

In the context of ENISA’s IPR policy, the general principle is free access under open licenses. The policy encompasses all types of IP assets and rights that are applicable for ENISA. It covers both protection of ENISA’s IPR, as well as protection of the IPR of (third) parties involved in ENISA’s work. It also includes information about the IPR in ENISA’s contracts and other agreements, as well as IPR in ENISA’s working groups.

You can download the public version of ENISA's IPR Polcy from here.

For further information regarding ENISA’s IPR policy, please contact: info@enisa.europa.eu
